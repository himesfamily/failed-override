# Mission 2 Intro Cinematic Design

## Goal

Play the supplied debriefing scene after the player confirms the Mission 2 crew and before the Cargo Bay battle begins.

## Presentation

- Use a new Aurora debriefing-room illustration that matches the existing cinematic art.
- Show Captain Gershon, Issac, Xander, Nance, Akiko, and Dr. Chrandy Perra gathered in the room.
- Chrandy is a silent cameo. Do not show the twins or Tiger yet.
- Use the existing speaker portraits and dialogue-panel style for each speaking beat.
- Split Issac's long explanation across several clicks without changing its meaning.
- Preserve click, Space/Enter, and Skip controls used by the Mission 1 cinematic.

## Ending Transition

After Xander warns that another wave is arriving, fade to black and show an intrusion alert identifying the Aurora Cargo Bay. One more click begins Mission 2 and switches to battle music.

## Campaign Flow

The cinematic runs after crew preparation, so the selected roster and progression choices are already saved. Mission 2's battle state is not created until the cinematic finishes or is skipped. Replaying Mission 2 shows the intro again.

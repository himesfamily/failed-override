# Mission 2 Security-Center Passage Design

## Intent

Mission 2 takes place aboard the hostile ship, so its destination is a passage into the security center rather than an evacuation shuttle back to the Aurora.

## Behavior

- The upper-right objective is labeled and drawn as the security-center passage.
- Moving any deployed crew member onto the passage immediately completes Mission 2. Summoned and reprogrammed machines cannot complete the story objective by themselves.
- Entering costs movement but does not require a separate interaction action.
- The entering crew member earns the existing 20 XP objective award.
- Issac and at least one hero must still survive, preserving Mission 2's established loss conditions.
- Mission titles, instructions, event text, and continuity wording no longer describe Mission 2 as an evacuation.

## Approach

Rename the mission-definition objective, complete the objective from the normal movement path, and render a clear native security-center marker. This replaces the old Issac-only adjacent interaction and avoids introducing a second special movement system.

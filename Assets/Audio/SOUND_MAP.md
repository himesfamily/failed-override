# Failed Override Audio Set

These sounds were created specifically for the Failed Override prototype. They are original procedural audio and may be used, changed, and distributed with the game.

## Music bus

- `landing_theme_selected.mp3` — selected landing theme downloaded from Pixabay.
- `battle_theme_selected.mp3` — selected battle theme downloaded from Pixabay.
- `landing_theme_v2.wav` — warm, hopeful title-screen theme; designed to loop.
- `battle_theme_v2.wav` — cinematic tactical-combat theme; designed to loop.
- `ompok_motif_v2.wav` — ominous theme for Emperor Ompok; designed to loop.
- `landing_theme.wav`, `battle_theme.wav`, `ompok_motif.wav` — original retro-style drafts retained for comparison.
- `victory_cue.wav` — mission victory stinger.
- `defeat_cue.wav` — mission failure stinger.

## Ambience bus

- `ship_ambience.wav` — friendly ship interiors; designed to loop.
- `laboratory_ambience.wav` — research outpost interiors; designed to loop.

## Effects bus

- `ui_click.wav`, `ui_hover.wav` — menus and action buttons.
- `footstep.wav`, `droid_move.wav` — unit movement.
- `plasma_pistol.wav`, `vibro_axe.wav` — Xander's attacks.
- `claw_attack.wav`, `maul_attack.wav`, `bear_transform.wav` — Nance's actions.
- `robot_summon.wav` — Akiko's summon action.
- `reprogram.wav` — Issac's reprogram action.
- `heal.wav`, `stun_pistol.wav` — Chrandy's actions.
- `stun_ray.wav`, `saw_blade.wav`, `heavy_droid_attack.wav` — enemy attacks.
- `damage_hit.wav`, `unit_defeat.wav` — combat results.
- `rescue.wav`, `door_console.wav` — objectives and interactions.

The version 2 themes are stereo, 16-bit PCM WAV at 32,000 Hz. The remaining files are mono, 16-bit PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz. Themes and ambience should loop; effects and cues should play once.
